Raiding

Two scheduled nights a week with a set roster, clear strats, and a fair loot system. We push progress without burning anyone out.

  • Sign-ups open a week ahead
  • Strats posted before every new boss
  • Bench spots rotate fairly

PvP events

Weekly battleground premades, world PvP outings, and the occasional guild duel tournament with gold prizes.

  • Premade battlegrounds on Wednesdays
  • Monthly duel tournament
  • New PvPers paired with a veteran

Leveling help

New to the game or rolling an alt? Ask in guild chat and someone will group up, run you through dungeons, or answer questions.

How raid night works

  1. Sign up in DiscordReact to the raid post by Sunday so leads can build the roster.
  2. Prep your characterBring consumables, repair, and skim the strat post for any new boss.
  3. Invites go out 15 minutes earlyBe online and in voice. We pull on time.
  4. Loot is handed out fairlyWe use a points system that rewards attendance. The full rules are pinned in Discord.

The guild code

  • Be friendly. No toxicity in chat, voice, or random groups. You wear our tag everywhere.
  • Competitive, not cutthroat. We want to win and improve, and we laugh at wipes.
  • Help when you can. A five-minute answer for a new player builds the guild.
  • Real life comes first. Can't make raid? Just let an officer know.
  • Respect the guild bank. Take what you need and give back when you can.

Officers

Message any of us in-game or on Discord.

Questions

Do I need to be max level to join?

No. We take players at any level, and leveling help is one of the main reasons the guild exists. Raids need max level, but everything else is open to all.

How much do I have to play?

As much or as little as you want. Only raiders on the main roster are asked to attend, and even then we work around real life.

I've never raided or done PvP. Is that okay?

Yes. We explain fights in voice, pair new PvPers with veterans, and nobody gets flamed for learning.

Do I need a microphone?

You'll need to be able to listen in Discord for raids and PvP premades. Talking is optional.

How do I get an invite?

Fill out the application below, copy it, and post it in our Discord's application channel. An officer will reply within a day or two and invite you in-game.
